About CraftVerify

Why we built this and what we're actually trying to do.

This started with frustration.

Every resident in Redemption City has a story. A plumber who took the deposit and never came back. An electrician who wired something wrong and left before the problem showed. A painter who quoted one price and charged another.

The system that exists right now is a WhatsApp group and a prayer. Someone asks, someone recommends, you call a stranger and hope. There is no record. There is no accountability. There is no way to know.

CraftVerify was built because that frustration deserves a real answer — not just a better WhatsApp group, but a platform where trust is earned, recorded, and visible.

What we are.

CraftVerify is a verified artisan marketplace built specifically for Redemption City. We connect residents with skilled tradespeople who have been checked — by identity, by skill, and by track record.

We are not a job board. We are not a social network. We are a trust infrastructure for a community that deserves one.

What we are not.

We are not a platform that lets anyone sign up and call themselves verified. We are not interested in scale for its own sake. We are not building another directory of unverified strangers.

Every person on this platform has been manually reviewed. That will slow our growth. We are fine with that.

Where we're going.

Redemption City is the beginning. The artisan trust problem exists in every Nigerian neighbourhood — Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan. CraftVerify is being built to scale carefully, community by community, without sacrificing the verification standard that makes it worth using.

The goal is not to be the biggest platform. The goal is to be the most trusted one.

"If getting verified were easy, the badge would mean nothing."
