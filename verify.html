How We Verify

Getting listed on CraftVerify is not easy. That's deliberate.

A verified badge on this platform means one thing: someone at CraftVerify has physically confirmed that this person is who they say they are, can do what they claim to do, and has done it before — to someone else's satisfaction.

Not a form. Not a fee. Not a selfie. A real check.

01

Identity

Every artisan must present a valid NIN — National Identification Number — tied to a real name, a real face, and a real phone number. We cross-check. We call. We confirm it's actually them.

Why this matters: In Nigeria, accountability starts with identity. An artisan who gives you their real name and NIN cannot disappear.

02

Skill

Claiming a skill is not enough. Every artisan must show evidence — a trade certificate, a NABTEB qualification, or a portfolio of completed work with photos. Where neither exists, we conduct a practical skills assessment.

Why this matters: Anyone can say they're an electrician. Not everyone can wire a distribution board correctly. We find out before you hire them.

03

Track Record

Every applicant must provide at least two verifiable past clients — people we can call, not names on a form. We ask three questions: Did they show up? Did they do the job? Would you hire them again?

Why this matters: Past behaviour is the most reliable predictor of future behaviour. Two honest references tell us more than a hundred self-written reviews.

04

Community Vouching

Where possible, we seek endorsement from community leaders or existing verified artisans within Redemption City. Someone who is already trusted vouching for someone new carries real cultural weight — and real accountability.

Why this matters: Trust is social before it is digital. A voucher puts their own reputation on the line.

What verification is NOT.

Paying a fee. Filling a form. Having a smartphone. Uploading a photo. These are barriers — not filters. They screen out poor artisans and rich fraudsters equally badly.

The friction is the product. If getting verified were easy, the badge would mean nothing.
